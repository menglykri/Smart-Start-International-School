About Smart Start

Rooted in Cambodia, connected to the world, and committed to every child’s potential.

Our story

A school built around belonging.

Smart Start International School began with a simple belief: children flourish when academic challenge, creativity and care live together.

Our kindergarten and primary community brings families and educators together around shared values—curiosity, kindness, courage and responsibility.
