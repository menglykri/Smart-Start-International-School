Learning beyond the classroom

A local ecosystem became an open-air classroom as students investigated the living world through observation, questions and teamwork.

Armed with notebooks and plenty of curiosity, learners recorded plants, insects and changing conditions around them. Teachers guided the experience with prompts, but students led the discoveries—comparing evidence, sketching what they saw and discussing why each detail mattered.

“The best learning happens when children can connect an idea to something real.”

Back at school, each team transformed its field notes into a visual project. The activity brought science, language and collaboration together while giving every student a meaningful role.

Reading week brings stories alive

Favourite characters stepped off the page during a joyful week of storytelling, shared reading and imagination.

Classrooms welcomed guest readers, students exchanged book recommendations and families joined the celebration from home. Each day offered a different way to connect with stories—from expressive read-aloud sessions to costumes inspired by much-loved characters.

Every child had a story to share—and a new story waiting to be discovered.

The week strengthened reading confidence while showing that books are more than schoolwork. They are invitations to wonder, empathise and see the world through someone else’s eyes.

Young artists exhibition

Colour, culture and imagination filled our campus as student artwork took centre stage in the annual creative showcase.

From bold paintings to mixed-media sculptures, every piece told a personal story. Students explored local patterns, nature and everyday life before developing their own ideas through sketching, experimentation and thoughtful feedback.

“Art gives children another language for expressing what they notice, feel and imagine.”

Families toured the exhibition with young artists as their guides. The pride on each child’s face made the evening special—and reminded us that the creative process matters just as much as the finished work.
